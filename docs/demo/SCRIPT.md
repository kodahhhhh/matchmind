# Demo video script

`matchpulse-demo.mp4`: 2 min 59 s, 1440x900, 25 fps, H.264 + AAC, burned-in captions.

Narration: ElevenLabs, voice George (eleven_multilingual_v2). Every scene is a real screen recording at 1440x900 of the app running on the `ws/W5-more-matches` data (2,924 matches, 67 competitions) against the live API, driven by Playwright.

| Time | Scene | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Cold open | Every football argument ends the same way. Someone says "but they had sixty-two percent possession", and everyone goes home angry. Stats apps tell you what happened. MatchPulse tells you why. |
| 0:12 | Home page, 2,924 matches | Meet MatchPulse. Two thousand nine hundred and twenty-four matches you can replay: the big five leagues, two World Cups, two Euros, and every Messi season at Barcelona. Every one scored by models we trained ourselves. Let's open the 2022 World Cup final. |
| 0:30 | Match view and highlights | Argentina, France, three three, and a billion people needing a lie-down. Every pass, carry and shot on the pitch. Momentum, chances and every goal on the timeline. Press play, and the highlights roll. |
| 0:43 | Find the turning point and the analyst | Now the big button. Find the turning point. Our change-point model scans momentum minute by minute, and the analyst explains it. Every number comes from our tools, never from the language model. If it can't back a claim with a real event, it doesn't get to say it. Tap a citation and the pitch jumps straight there. Eightieth minute, Mbappé, penalty. France fans, you can skip ahead. |
| 1:08 | Most dangerous moments | The Moments tab ranks every attack by how close it actually came to a goal. Hit play and it unfolds pass by pass, with live commentary written only from the event data. No hallucinated wonder-strikes. |
